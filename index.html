01 — About the center

A field station for a changing coastline

Read the full profile →

Pusat Kajian Mitigasi Bencana dan Rehabilitasi Pesisir (PKMBRP) Indonesian name for CoREM · affiliated with Indonesia's Center of Excellence (PUI) network

CoREM was established at {{ site.institution }} to study coastal disaster risk and support the rehabilitation of degraded shorelines along Central Java's north coast — a region where land subsidence, tidal flooding, and mangrove loss compound each other year over year.

The center combines in-situ field survey and water sampling, satellite and drone-based remote sensing, and geospatial modeling — applied to real, named places, not abstractions. Pekalongan's batik-industry estuaries. Demak's disappearing villages. Pemalang's replanted mangrove belt.
