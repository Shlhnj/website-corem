<div class="full-name">
  Pusat Kajian Mitigasi Bencana dan Rehabilitasi Pesisir (PKMBRP)
  <span>Indonesian name for CoREM · affiliated with Indonesia's Center of Excellence (PUI) network</span>
</div>

CoREM was established at Universitas Diponegoro to study coastal disaster risk and support the rehabilitation of degraded shorelines along Central Java's north coast — a region where land subsidence, tidal flooding, and mangrove loss compound each other year over year.

The center works toward becoming a reference point for coastal disaster and mitigation studies in Indonesia and internationally, producing research that is meant to be used: vulnerability indices, shoreline forecasts, and rehabilitation designs that local governments and communities can act on.

In practice, that means combining three kinds of work under one roof: in-situ field survey and water sampling, satellite and drone-based remote sensing, and geospatial modeling — applied to real, named places, not abstractions. Pekalongan's batik-industry estuaries. Demak's disappearing villages. Pemalang's replanted mangrove belt.

CoREM also trains graduate researchers through this fieldwork, and partners with institutions beyond Java — including a 2023 flood-vulnerability study on the coastal plain of Suriname, and collaborative work with Hasanuddin University and Gadjah Mada University within Indonesia.
