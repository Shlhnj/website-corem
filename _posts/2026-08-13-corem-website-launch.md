CoREM's research has, until now, lived only in journal archives and departmental listings scattered across Universitas Diponegoro's systems. This site brings the center's mission, research programs, and publication record together in one place for the first time.

The publication list currently draws on public journal records rather than an internal archive. If you're part of the lab and spot a gap, edit `_data/publications.yml` in this site's repository to add it.
